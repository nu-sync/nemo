For tonight's dinner, start by warming a little olive oil in a wide pan. Add the chopped onion and let it soften slowly while you rinse the rice. Stir in the garlic only after the onion becomes translucent, because garlic burns quickly. Pour in the warm stock a little at a time and keep stirring gently. Meanwhile, roast the mushrooms on a separate tray so their edges become crisp instead of steaming in the pan. When the rice is nearly tender, fold in the peas and a small handful of grated cheese. Taste before adding salt. Serve the mushrooms on top, with lemon wedges on the side. If anyone arrives late, keep their portion warm with a splash of stock rather than leaving it over direct heat. Put the remaining cheese in a small bowl on the table.
