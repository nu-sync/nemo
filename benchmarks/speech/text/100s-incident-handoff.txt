This is the handoff for the delayed report incident. The application remained available throughout the morning, but several scheduled reports arrived later than expected. The first alert described a growing queue rather than a complete failure. We checked the database connection pool and the worker logs before changing anything. Both showed that workers were waiting on a slow external request, not on a locked table.

We reduced the timeout for that request and restarted one worker as a controlled test. The queue began to shrink without an increase in errors, so we applied the same change to the remaining workers. Reports that were already running were allowed to finish. We did not delete queued work or ask customers to submit their requests again. The support team has a list of the affected accounts and a draft explanation, but nothing has been sent yet.

For the next shift, please watch the oldest pending job and the number of retries. A falling queue length by itself is not enough evidence that delivery has recovered. We need to confirm that completed reports actually appear in the customer portal and contain the expected date range. If the external service slows down again, pause the optional enrichment step rather than disabling the entire report pipeline. Tomorrow we should add a test for this failure mode and decide whether the enrichment task belongs in a separate queue. Keep the temporary timeout change visible in the incident notes until that decision is made.
